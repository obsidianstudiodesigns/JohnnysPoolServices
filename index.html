Questions people ask

Do you do pool repairs in Cape Town?

Yes. John repairs leaks, cracked fittings, pumps, filters, plumbing and pool lights, and renovates tired pools all over Cape Town and the Western Cape.

Which areas do you cover?

John works all over Cape Town and the Western Cape, including the City Bowl, Atlantic Seaboard, Southern and Northern Suburbs, Somerset West, Strand, Gordon's Bay, Stellenbosch, Paarl, Wellington and Franschhoek. If your area isn't listed, ask.

Can you fix a green pool?

Yes. Green pool turnarounds are part of John's pool cleaning and water care. He finds out why the pool turned, whether it's the chemistry, the pump or the filter, and fixes that too.

How much does a pool repair cost?

Every pool is different, so John quotes after he has seen the pool and the equipment. You get the price before any work starts.

Do you offer regular pool cleaning?

Yes. John offers regular or once-off visits: brushing, vacuuming, skimming, water testing and balancing the chemicals.

Do you install and repair solar pool heating?

Yes. Solar heating mats are installed, extended and repaired, including leaks and pipework on the roof. Heat pump options are available if you want to swim in winter too.

When can I reach you?

Johnny's Pool Services is open every day. Call or WhatsApp 061 765 8479, or email johnvdbergmsp@gmail.com.
